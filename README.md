# Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data from the Superstore dataset using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The objective was to identify sales trends, profitability drivers, regional performance, customer segment behavior, and forecast future sales performance through interactive dashboards.

## Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Evaluate regional and customer segment performance
* Understand the impact of discounts on profitability
* Discover seasonal sales trends
* Forecast future sales performance

## Tools & Technologies

* Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
* MySQL
* Power BI
* DAX
* Jupyter Notebook

## Project Workflow

### Data Preparation

* Loaded and explored the Superstore dataset
* Cleaned and transformed data using Python
* Processed date fields and handled data quality checks
* Exported cleaned data for analysis

### SQL Analysis

* Revenue and profit analysis
* Category and regional performance analysis
* Top-performing product analysis
* Customer segment analysis
* Ranking and CTE-based queries

### Power BI Dashboard Development

#### Page 1: Executive Sales Overview

* Business KPIs
* Monthly Revenue Trend
* Regional Sales Performance
* Profit by Product Category
* Customer Segment Sales Contribution

#### Page 2: Product & Profit Analysis

* Top 10 Most Profitable Products
* Profit Analysis by Sub-Category
* Discount Impact on Profitability
* Product Performance Summary

#### Page 3: Forecast & Trend Analysis

* Sales Trend with Forecast
* Annual Sales Growth Comparison
* Seasonal Sales Trends by Month
* Monthly Profit Trend Analysis

## Key Insights

* Technology generated the highest profit
* West region produced the highest profit
* Central region generated the lowest profit
* Consumer segment contributed over 50% of total sales
* Higher discount levels negatively impacted profitability
* Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the most profitable product
